Support

For help with Furrecord, open a support issue. Dev Team reviews questions and bug reports there. A GitHub account is required to post.

Include your app version, iOS version and a short description. Issues are public: do not include pet records, payment information or other sensitive details.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account used for your purchase. Manage or cancel a yearly subscription in your App Store account settings.

Reminders

Allow notifications in iOS Settings and check that reminders are enabled for the relevant care entry. Reminders require Pro.

iCloud

iCloud sync is optional and requires Pro. Enable it in Furrecord settings while signed into iCloud. The app may ask you to reopen it to apply a sync change.
