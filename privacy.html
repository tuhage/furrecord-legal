Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Dev Team does not collect data through Furrecord. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or a Furrecord account.

Your pet records

Pet profiles, photos and care records are stored on your device. If you choose to enable iCloud sync with Furrecord Pro, records are synchronized through your private iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. Dev Team does not receive or access these records.

Permissions and purchases

Photos you select are used for pet profiles. Notifications, if allowed, are scheduled locally for care reminders. Apple processes purchases and provides purchase status so the app can enable Pro features. Dev Team does not receive your payment details.

Your choices

You can delete pets and their records in the app, disable iCloud sync in settings, and change notification permissions in system settings. Turning sync off does not itself delete copies already in iCloud. You choose whether and with whom to share exported PDF summaries.

Website and support

This public website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Visiting it or opening a support issue is subject to GitHub’s privacy statement. Support issues are public. Do not post private records, payment information or other sensitive details.
