Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Dev Team does not collect data through Furrecord. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, or a Furrecord account.

Your pet records

Pet profiles, photos and care records are stored only on your device. Dev Team does not receive or access these records.

Permissions and purchases

Photos you select are used for pet profiles. Notifications, if allowed, are scheduled locally for care reminders. Apple processes purchases and provides purchase status so the app can enable Pro features. Dev Team does not receive your payment details.

Your choices

You can delete pets and their records in the app and change notification permissions in system settings. You choose whether and with whom to share exported PDF summaries.

Website and support

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub processes website requests under its privacy statement. If you email support, Dev Team uses the information you choose to send to respond to your request. Do not send private pet records or payment details.

Questions: furrecordhelp@sent.com. See Support for help.
